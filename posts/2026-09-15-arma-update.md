## House Financial Services Committee schedules markup for ARMA 2026 (H.R. 8957)  (importance 7/10)

The House Financial Services Committee will hold a markup on the American Reserve Modernization Act of 2026 (H.R. 8957, the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve bill) on September 16, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. ET. The bill, introduced May 21, 2026, by Rep. Nick Begich (R-AK) with Rep. Jared Golden (D-ME) as co-lead and 23 total cosponsors, remains in committee. It would codify a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve using primarily forfeited Bitcoin, impose a 20-year lockup on holdings (with limited exceptions after for debt reduction), require quarterly third-party audited proof-of-reserve reports, establish secure Treasury custody within 180 days of enactment, create a separate Digital Asset Stockpile for non-Bitcoin assets, and direct study of budget-neutral acquisition methods (including potential gold certificate revaluation). This is the first scheduled committee action since introduction and a July 17, 2026, subcommittee hearing.

> **Why it matters:** Committee markup is a major procedural step that advances the bill out of stasis; qualifies as scheduled committee action (7-8 range) and is the first real movement since May introduction.
