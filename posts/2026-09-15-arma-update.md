## House Financial Services Committee schedules markup of ARMA 2026 (H.R. 8957) for September 16, 2026  (importance 8/10)

The House Financial Services Committee will hold a full committee markup of the American Reserve Modernization Act of 2026 on September 16 at 10:00 a.m. ET. This is the first full-committee action on the bill since introduction; it involves debate, possible amendments, and a committee vote on whether to advance it to the House floor. The bill would codify a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, impose a 20-year lockup on holdings (primarily from forfeitures), require quarterly third-party audited proof-of-reserve reports, prohibit new debt/taxes/deficit spending for acquisitions, direct a study of budget-neutral mechanisms (including gold certificate revaluation and Fed remittances), and create a separate Digital Asset Stockpile. It has 23 cosponsors (22 Republicans, 1 Democrat: Rep. Jared Golden).

> **Why it matters:** First substantive committee action on the bill since introduction, fitting the 7-8 tier for a scheduled committee markup with potential vote.
