## ARMA Bill Added to Congressional Hearing Agenda  (importance 6/10)

H.R. 8957 (American Reserve Modernization Act), introduced by Rep. Nick Begich with over 20 bipartisan co-sponsors, was formally listed for discussion at the July 17 House Financial Services field hearing alongside the CLARITY Act and H.Res. 111. The bill authorizes Treasury to acquire Bitcoin via budget-neutral mechanisms funded in part through gold revaluation accounting, mandates secure custody, audits, and 20-year holding periods.
