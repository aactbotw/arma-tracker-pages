## H.R. 8957 (ARMA 2026) Formally Reported by House Financial Services Committee and Placed on Union Calendar  (importance 7/10)

The American Reserve Modernization Act of 2026 (H.R. 8957), which establishes a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and a separate Digital Asset Stockpile in the Treasury, was formally reported with amendments as House Report 119-894 on November 24, 2026, and placed on the Union Calendar as Calendar No. 789. This makes the bill eligible for a House floor vote. The committee had previously ordered it reported by a 28-21 vote on September 16, 2026. The reported version includes a 20-year lock-up on Bitcoin placed in the reserve (no selling, swapping, auctioning, encumbering, or disposal), requires proof-of-reserve reporting with independent audits, and directs a study of budget-neutral mechanisms for additional Bitcoin acquisition (via swaps of other assets, future forfeitures, settlements, or partnerships; it explicitly excludes new taxes, borrowing, or deficit spending). Amended text removed earlier references to studying Federal Reserve remittances or gold certificate revaluation as funding options.

> **Why it matters:** Formal committee reporting and calendar placement is a major procedural advancement that moves the bill closer to a potential floor vote, though it falls short of an actual markup result or floor action.
