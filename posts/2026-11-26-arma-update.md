## H.R. 8957 (ARMA 2026) Reported by House Financial Services Committee and Placed on Union Calendar  (importance 8/10)

The American Reserve Modernization Act of 2026 (H.R. 8957), which establishes a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and Digital Asset Stockpile, was formally reported as amended (H. Rept. 119-894) by the House Committee on Financial Services on November 24, 2026, and placed on the Union Calendar, Calendar No. 789. This makes the bill eligible for consideration by the full House. The committee had previously ordered it reported by a 28-21 vote on September 16, 2026. The reported bill requires transfer of government Bitcoin to the reserve with a minimum 20-year holding period during which it cannot be sold, swapped, auctioned, or encumbered. It also mandates proof-of-reserve reporting and a study on budget-neutral methods to potentially acquire additional Bitcoin.

> **Why it matters:** Formal committee reporting and placement on the Union Calendar is a major procedural milestone that advances the bill toward a potential floor vote.
