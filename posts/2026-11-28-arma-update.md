The American Reserve Modernization Act of 2026 (H.R. 8957), which would establish a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and a separate Digital Asset Stockpile within 180 days of enactment, was formally reported by the House Financial Services Committee on November 24, 2026 as House Report 119-894 and placed on the Union Calendar (Calendar No. 789), making it eligible for a floor vote.
