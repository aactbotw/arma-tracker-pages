## H.R. 8957 (American Reserve Modernization Act of 2026 / ARMA) formally reported to full House  (importance 6/10)

The House Financial Services Committee officially reported the amended bill (House Report 119-894) on November 24, 2026, and it was placed on the Union Calendar as Calendar No. 789, making it eligible for a House floor vote. The bill codifies the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, requires Treasury to establish the reserve and a Digital Asset Stockpile within 180 days of enactment, mandates a minimum 20-year holding period during which reserve Bitcoin cannot be sold, swapped, auctioned, encumbered, or disposed of, requires proof-of-reserve reporting and audits, and directs a study on budget-neutral strategies for additional acquisitions (explicitly barring new taxes, borrowing, or deficit spending). Amended text removed earlier references to gold certificate revaluation or Federal Reserve remittances as funding mechanisms.

> **Why it matters:** Significant procedural advancement to full House eligibility; not a markup result, hearing, or vote so scores at top of procedural range.

## Amended ARMA 2026 removes gold revaluation as potential funding mechanism  (importance 4/10)

The committee-reported version of H.R. 8957 eliminates the original proposal to study using gold certificate revaluation or Federal Reserve remittances to fund Bitcoin purchases. The revised study focuses on swapping other digital assets, forfeitures/settlements, and state/private partnerships, while explicitly prohibiting borrowing, new taxes, or deficit spending.
